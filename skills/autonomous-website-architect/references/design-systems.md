# Design System Archetypes & Visual Directions

When designing websites autonomously without a user-supplied reference, select a coherent aesthetic archetype matching the product's identity. Avoid generic SaaS defaults unless explicitly building enterprise software.

---

## 1. The 7 Visual Archetypes

### 1. Gaming & Immersive Experiences
- **Vibe**: Dark, cinematic, high atmospheric tension, warm torchlight or neon glows.
- **Palette**: Deep void blacks (`#080604`), rich dark umber, burnt orange (`#ff8a2b`), glowing amber (`#ffb547`), gold coins.
- **Typography**: Ultra-condensed display headlines (*Bebas Neue*, *Barlow Condensed*), robust neutral body (*Barlow*, *Rajdhani*).
- **Geometry**: Slanted polygon corners (`clip-path: polygon(...)`), double-cut inset borders, textured grain (`mix-blend-mode: overlay`), rising particle embers.
- **UI Elements**: HUD metric counters, rank badges, frame-by-frame sprite sheets, interactive audio cues, treasure cards.

### 2. Crypto, Web3 & FinTech
- **Vibe**: Futuristic, clean mathematical precision, high-contrast digital elegance.
- **Palette**: Midnight navy/slate (`#06080e`, `#0f172a`), cyan electric blue (`#38bdf8`), deep violet (`#a855f7`), frosted glass.
- **Typography**: Geometric display (*Syne*, *Space Grotesk*), monospaced figures (*JetBrains Mono*).
- **Geometry**: Pill-shaped badges, subtle 1px glassmorphism borders (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`), radial gradient spotlights.
- **UI Elements**: Live charts (SVG sparklines), wallet connect states, token contract copy pills with feedback, live transaction logs.

### 3. Modern SaaS & Developer Platforms
- **Vibe**: Clean, confident, high legibility, modular clarity.
- **Palette**: Dark carbon (`#0a0d14`), indigo-violet brand accent (`#6366f1`), crisp sky secondary (`#38bdf8`), cool slate text.
- **Typography**: Modern geometric neo-grotesque (*Plus Jakarta Sans*, *Inter*).
- **Geometry**: Rounded cards (`12px` to `16px`), subtle inner borders (`box-shadow: inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255,255,255,0.1)`), soft ambient blur glows.
- **UI Elements**: Interactive feature tabs, interactive pricing toggles (Monthly/Annual), terminal / code snippet blocks, customer testimonial grids.

### 4. Luxury & Editorial E-Commerce
- **Vibe**: High fashion, refined craftsmanship, calm premium breathing room.
- **Palette**: Warm basalt (`#0c0a09`), champagne gold (`#d4af37`), ivory cream (`#fafaf9`), muted stone (`#a8a29e`).
- **Typography**: High-contrast modern serif (*Playfair Display*, *Cormorant Garamond*), clean geometric body (*Outfit*, *Manrope*).
- **Geometry**: Minimal borders, generous vertical padding (`120px+`), clean grid lines, full-bleed hero imagery.
- **UI Elements**: Filter drawers, quick-view product modals, cart slide-outs, high-res hover zoom.

### 5. Creative Portfolio & Studio
- **Vibe**: Bold, typographic, brutalist or hyper-editorial, memorable personality.
- **Palette**: Pure black (`#000000`), stark white (`#ffffff`), electric punch accent (hot pink `#ff3366` or lime `#ccff00`).
- **Typography**: Expressive display sans (*Clash Display*, *Cabinet Grotesk*), neutral body (*Satoshi*, *General Sans*).
- **Geometry**: Large corner radii (`24px+`) or strict raw borders, massive display sizes (`clamp(64px, 15vw, 180px)`), smooth marquee bands.
- **UI Elements**: Interactive project showcases, cursor hover effects, magnetic buttons, filterable archive tables.

### 6. Operations & Data Dashboards
- **Vibe**: High data density, instant scanning, functional productivity.
- **Palette**: Deep slate (`#0b0f19`), surface card (`#111827`), primary cobalt (`#2563eb`), status emerald (`#10b981`), amber warning (`#f59e0b`).
- **Typography**: Standard system/Inter (*Inter*, *JetBrains Mono* for tabular numbers).
- **Geometry**: Compact 8px–10px radii, clear 1px dividing lines, tabbed viewports.
- **UI Elements**: Filter toolbars, paginated data tables, KPI metric badges with percent trends, status tags.

### 7. Enterprise & Corporate Business
- **Vibe**: Trusted, stable, conversion-oriented, clear value propositions.
- **Palette**: Deep corporate navy (`#090d16`), trusted sapphire blue (`#0ea5e9`), crisp white text, silver borders.
- **Typography**: Strong corporate headline (*Cabinet Grotesk*, *DM Sans*).
- **Geometry**: Structured 12px rounded cards, solid button weights, high contrast.
- **UI Elements**: Multi-step quote forms, compliance trust badges, client logo carousels, service breakdowns.
